Deulli (들리) is a mobile app for Korean speakers learning English through podcasts. It combines podcast listening with English transcripts, Korean text translations and dialogue replay. Deulli is available on iOS and Android.

This is Deulli's official product introduction. [한국어 소개](/about/)

## Follow the words you hear

Listen to an English podcast, check a sentence in the English transcript, and use the Korean translation when you need help with its meaning. Replay that dialogue to compare the written words with the audio. The learning player also provides playback speed controls, shadowing practice, vocabulary tools and quizzes. Check the latest app for the availability and conditions of individual features.

Deulli is for learners who want to work through recorded audio with text support. If you need live conversation lessons or a course for a particular exam, check those requirements separately before choosing an app.

## Korean text translation and spoken language selection

Korean subtitles translate the words on the screen. Spoken language selection is a separate feature in supported Deulli official content: listeners can choose Korean or English for each host. This does not mean that every external podcast can be converted to host-by-host bilingual audio.

The [official podcast selection](/podcasts/) introduces three public Deulli episodes about English listening practice, history and everyday science. That selection and the linked episode descriptions are in Korean. Open an episode's share page for its details and the app link; listening and study take place in the app.

## iPhone lock-screen subtitles

Deulli's iPhone lock-screen player provides subtitles and previous/next dialogue buttons. This lets listeners check the text or move between dialogues without reopening the full app. Check the current iPhone app for supported devices and usage conditions. This description does not claim the same lock-screen features on Android.

## Create a podcast about a topic

The app also has a podcast creation feature for topics you want to listen to. Check the creation feature in the app's learning menu for current availability and usage conditions.
