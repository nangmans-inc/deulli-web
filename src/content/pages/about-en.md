Deulli (들리) is a mobile app for Korean speakers learning English through podcasts. Its core is Weekly, a seven-day listening course built on podcasts about a topic you choose. Every episode comes with an English transcript, Korean text translation and dialogue replay. Deulli runs on iOS and Android.

This is Deulli's official product introduction.

## Weekly: a seven-day course on your topic

Tell Deulli what you want to hear about this week. Deulli makes two podcasts on that topic and splits them into daily missions. You spend three days on each podcast:

1. **Listen to the whole story.** Sound comes before text. Afterwards you write what you understood in your own words.
2. **Listen again in short sections.** Open the transcript, the translation or dictation only where you need them.
3. **Review the sentences you missed.** Sentences you got wrong on the first two days come back. Depending on your results, shadowing practice follows.

One mission unlocks each day. On day seven you look back at the week's record and choose next week's topic.

Your first Weekly course can start as a free trial. Continuing requires a Deulli Premium subscription. The app's subscription screen shows the trial period and the price.

## Follow the words you hear

In any episode you can read the English transcript as it plays and switch on the Korean translation when a sentence is unclear. In transcripts with timing, tap a line to replay from that point. Press and hold an English word to see its meaning and save it. You can save sentences too.

The learning player has playback speed and previous/next dialogue controls. The learning menu holds review of saved expressions, quizzes and shadowing. Quizzes and shadowing open on episodes prepared for them.

Deulli has no live conversation lessons with a tutor.

## Korean text translation and spoken language selection

These are two separate features. Korean subtitles translate the words on the screen. Spoken language selection exists in supported Deulli official episodes: you choose Korean or English for each host. External podcasts do not all have it.

The [official podcast selection](/podcasts/) introduces three public Deulli episodes about English listening practice, history and everyday science. That page and the episode descriptions are in Korean. Listening and study take place in the app.

## iPhone lock-screen subtitles

On iPhone, the lock-screen player shows subtitles and previous/next dialogue buttons, so you can read a line or move between dialogues without opening the app. Turn it on in the app's playback settings. This is an iPhone feature.

## Create a podcast about a topic

Separately from Weekly, the creation feature in the learning menu makes a podcast on a topic you type in.
